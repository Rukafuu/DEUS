# DEUS Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the DEUS community a respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and respect;
- give and accept constructive technical feedback;
- focus disagreements on ideas, evidence, and project outcomes;
- respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and security boundaries;
- take responsibility for mistakes and repair harm where possible; and
- help keep project spaces safe and productive.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory conduct;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation, plagiarism, or knowingly misleading attribution;
- pressuring anyone to disclose credentials, private data, or vulnerability
  details publicly; and
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all DEUS project spaces, including repositories,
issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and community events. It
also applies when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include sensitive personal information in a
public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as
practical. The maintainer may request context, remove content, issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant depending
on the severity and pattern of behavior.

Anyone handling a report must avoid conflicts of interest and protect the
privacy and safety of everyone involved. Retaliation against a person who makes
a good-faith report is prohibited.

## Enforcement guidelines

Enforcement should be proportional, consistent, and documented privately:

1. **Correction:** a private explanation and request to change behavior.
2. **Warning:** a formal warning with clear consequences for repetition.
3. **Temporary restriction:** limited participation for a defined period.
4. **Permanent ban:** removal from project spaces for severe or repeated harm.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and may remove contributions or comments that violate them.
