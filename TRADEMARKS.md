# DEUS Brand Usage

DEUS is an open source project. The source code is licensed separately from the
use of the DEUS name, logo, and official visual identity.

Reasonable descriptive use is welcome, including:

- “built with DEUS” and “compatible with DEUS”;
- articles, tutorials, examples, and integrations;
- forks that are clearly identified as modified or unofficial.

Please do not present a modified fork as an official DEUS release, imply an
affiliation with maintainers, or use official logos in a way that suggests
endorsement. Official assets in `assets/` and `editors/vscode/images/` remain
subject to applicable copyright and this policy.

This document makes no claim about trademark registration. Any future
registration or expanded brand policy requires a separate maintainer decision.
